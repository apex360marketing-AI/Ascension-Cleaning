Common questions

Everything you need to know.

How does the free quote process work?+

Submit your details and photos using the quote tool above. Our system generates a preliminary estimate instantly, then Kayla personally reviews your submission and sends a confirmed quote within 24 hours. No obligation — no pressure.

What's the difference between fixed price and hourly?+

A fixed price means you know the exact cost before we begin — nothing changes unless the scope does. Hourly billing tracks actual time at $80–100/hr for a 2-person team, and cannot guarantee a fixed end time. For most jobs, we recommend fixed price for peace of mind.

Do I need to be home during the clean?+

Not at all. Many clients provide a key or entry code and return to a spotless home. All team members are background-checked, bonded, and insured — your space is in trusted hands.

What areas do you serve?+

We primarily serve Kelowna and surrounding areas including West Kelowna, Lake Country, Peachland, and Rutland. Travel fees may apply beyond 20 km from central Kelowna — just ask and we'll quote you.

What if I'm not happy with the clean?+

Our 100% satisfaction guarantee means we return within 24 hours and re-clean any area that didn't meet the standard — no charge, no questions. Kayla follows up personally after every job, so any issue gets addressed immediately.

Do you supply your own equipment and products?+

Yes — we arrive fully equipped. Eco-friendly, non-toxic, plant-based products are available for all services at no extra charge. If you have specific product preferences or allergies, just let us know in your quote request.

Can I book online, or do I need a quote first?+

Standard home cleans, rental turnovers, window cleaning, and repeat visits can be booked directly on our calendar. Exterior work, specialty services, post-construction, and first-time deep cleans start with a free quote so we can price them accurately from your photos.

How does the referral program work?+

Share your personal code. When someone new books with Ascension and completes a clean of at least three hours, you receive a $50 credit and they get 10% off their first clean. The credit is only issued once their job is finished and paid. Shorter jobs and cancelled bookings don't qualify. There's no cap on how many credits you can earn.

Will I get reminders before my clean?+

Yes. You'll get an instant confirmation, a text 24 hours before, and a text when your crew is on the way. You can turn any of these off when you book or from your client portal.

Do you work with Airbnb and vacation rental hosts?+

Yes. We sync with your Airbnb or VRBO calendar, turn units over between checkout and check-in, send a photo report after every turnover, and offer linen service and restocking. Hosts with four or more turnovers a month save 10%.
